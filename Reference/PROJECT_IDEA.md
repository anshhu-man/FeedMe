# FeedMe — healthy cooking without the homework

**See a plate. Make it yours.**

It's late. You're hungry. A saved recipe asks for twelve ingredients and more energy than you have. FeedMe starts with a different question: what would make dinner manageable tonight?

A friend posts a wrap in Today. You tap **Make Mine**, choose the time and effort you can spare, and check what is already in your kitchen. FeedMe's intended experience offers a supported version with clear changes, short steps and realistic cleanup. Cook it, assemble it, simplify it—or pick something else.

When dinner is done, sharing is optional. Put a casual photo in **Today** for 24 hours. Keep a favorite on **My Plate** deliberately. Share with an invited **Kitchen Circle**, credit the original inspiration, swap a shortcut or save an author-permitted recipe for another night.

## The core loop

1. Find a real-life meal from your people or the reviewed library.
2. Tap Make Mine and confirm a version that fits your ingredients, time, equipment and energy.
3. Follow a manageable cooking or assembly flow; keep progress and save useful recipes.
4. Share your take if you want to, with source credit and the audience you choose.
5. Come back to meals that worked, without pressure to post or cook perfectly.

## What makes it FeedMe

- Practical, low-effort healthy cooking—not calorie guilt or a demand for perfection.
- A social format between fleeting stories and permanent posts: Today plus My Plate.
- Make Mine as the bridge from seeing somebody's food to actually making your own version.
- Kitchen Circles and voluntary invitations instead of compulsory public participation.
- Explicit safety, privacy, attribution, recipe permissions and content-review requirements.

The broader blueprint includes Fridge SOS, Tonight, Dinner Pact, Bring a Bit, Shortcut Swap, Dinner Vote and the remaining planned features. All **54 features and 98 screens** remain documented; phase labels describe execution order, not discarded scope.

## Concept versus current implementation

This is the product vision, not a claim that every feature works today. The native demo uses declared fixture variants rather than an AI adaptation service. Authentication, actual social delivery, nutrition/content review and production integrations remain unfinished. Never infer allergy-safe substitutions from a design or demo.

The project was previously named BiteClub; the [original fictional story](Brand/BiteClub_Brand_Story.md) is preserved as brand history. The active name is **FeedMe**. TasteEcho's earlier idea and the unrelated Devpost Bite Club entry are not part of this product.
