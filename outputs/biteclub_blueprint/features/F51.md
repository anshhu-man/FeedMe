# F51 — Content administration and professional review

## Strategy
A bounded, reviewed catalog is a production dependency for meal adaptation. Treat publishing as a versioned content release with evidence, rights and independent review, not free-form AI output. Staff tools are included in the screen and architecture maps because member features depend on them.

## Experience and screen design
ADMIN_LOGIN leads to ADMIN_HOME. ADMIN_RECIPE authors structured ingredients, amounts, steps, effort/equipment and provenance. ADMIN_REVIEW displays revision diff, review evidence and approval/publish/recall actions. ADMIN_SUBSTITUTION manages contextual ingredient/step transformations. ADMIN_PACK controls approved recipe manifests and store mapping. Each action identifies actor, target version and reason.

## Production sequence
Author saves a draft recipe revision. Submission freezes its content hash and declares reviewer requirements. An independently authorized reviewer records evidence and a decision tied to that hash. Publisher verifies required approvals, rights, tested timing evidence and schema/constraint fixtures before making the revision eligible. One transaction advances publication pointer and emits catalog revision events. Recall disables selection immediately and propagates to planners, cookbook state, caches and active sessions with an explicit read policy.

## Data and API contracts
Versioned recipe and substitution records, ingredients with normalized units/taxonomy, review_decisions, rights_evidence, recall_records and pack_revision manifests. API routes are `/v1/admin/recipes`, revision submit/reviews/publish/recall, `/v1/admin/substitutions` and `/v1/admin/packs`. Review evidence is not mutable through public/member endpoints. Plans pin exact recipe/substitution revisions plus rule-set version so an explanation can be reproduced.

## Permissions and lifecycle
Staff workforce SSO/MFA and backend RBAC. Authors cannot approve their own content; high-impact recalls/publish actions follow configured separation of duties. Credentials and scope of professional review are recorded and revalidated periodically. Community attachments are visibly unreviewed unless they pass the same workflow. Licensing must permit the planned recipe copy/adaptation use; author ownership of a food photo is separate from recipe-text rights.

## Failure and offline behavior
Concurrent draft edits use revision conflicts. An approval becomes stale when the content hash changes. No recall lease is issued by this API. An online catalog failure does not authorize new planning, publication or recipe copying from unverifiable state. A previously verified pinned cook may continue from the owner's local cache with its last-sync time visible; an offline client cannot know about a later server recall. A locally known recalled version is unavailable. Worker delay does not make a recalled revision eligible online because the database selection predicate changes synchronously. Staff authoring may keep an unsent draft locally; review and publish require online authorization.

## Integration and dependencies
F01/F08/F10 consume published reviewed transformations; F11 consumes tested preparation metadata; F46 consumes approved pack manifests. F54 owns recall/propagation telemetry and F49 rights/retention decisions. A production launch cannot treat this document or example recipes as completed professional review.

## Execution plan
Implement schema/migrations and fixtures; build author/reviewer roles and staff UI; add content hash signing/audit, deterministic eligibility tests, publish/recall outbox and client revision handling. Seed only content whose review and rights records are complete. Exercise full draft→reject→revise→approve→publish→recall workflow before public planning is enabled.

## Acceptance and release gates
Prove self-approval is rejected, changed content invalidates approval, recalled recipes never appear in new plans, private copies display recall state and unauthorized staff cannot publish. Measure review queue age, invalid eligible-content count (target zero), recall enforcement and provenance completeness. Roll back a bad publish pointer only through audited catalog operations; preserve historical pins for investigation.
