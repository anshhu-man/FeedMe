# F37 — Dinner Vote

## Strategy
Dinner Vote helps a circle make one bounded food decision. It does not infer consent to attend, purchase ingredients, or accept a Dinner Pact. Later delivery includes editable votes, deterministic closure, tie handling, and privacy-aware results rather than a decorative poll widget.

## Experience and screen design
`POLL_CREATE` accepts a short question, two distinct eligible-recipe options, a circle, and a closing time. The final preview identifies the audience and states “Results show totals, not who chose what.” `POLL_DETAIL` shows the choices, closing time, and the user’s current vote. A member can change or remove their vote while open. Totals appear after closure; a tie remains a tie. “Plan this” opens `TONIGHT` for the viewer, and a separate host action can open `PACT_CREATE` with a draft, requiring its own invitation confirmation.

## Production sequence
`POST /v1/polls` validates audience and options and commits `poll.created`. `POST /v1/polls/{pollId}/votes` with `{optionId,expectedVoteVersion}` locks the poll and viewer’s vote, checks server time and current eligibility, then upserts one vote plus `poll.vote_changed`. `DELETE /v1/polls/{pollId}/votes/me` removes it while open. A server-time-aware `GET /v1/polls/{pollId}` treats the poll as closed at the deadline even before the worker persists `poll.closed`. The close transaction freezes the eligible aggregate result once, using a unique close marker. `POST /v1/polls/{pollId}/close` supports explicit host closure or cancellation.

## Data and API contracts
`Poll(id,authorId,circleId,question,closesAt,state,version,resultVersion)` uses `OPEN`, `CLOSED`, `CANCELLED`. Published question, deadline, and `PollOption(id,pollId,label,recipeReference,position)` are immutable in this scope; corrections require closing and explicitly creating a new poll. `PollVote(pollId,userId,optionId,version,updatedAt)` has a unique poll/user constraint and foreign keys preventing votes for another poll’s option. The result contains eligible total counts and winning option IDs, never voter names. Typed errors include `POLL_CLOSED`, `OPTION_INVALID`, `OPTIONS_LOCKED`, and `VERSION_CONFLICT`.

## Permissions and lifecycle
Only current unblocked members of the selected circle may vote or view results. Votes from members who lose eligibility before closure are excluded from the final aggregate; the close transaction applies the same eligibility snapshot consistently. Closed totals remain aggregate historical results and do not reveal later individual membership changes. Source recipe links require independent access and never inherit new rights from an option. Participant counts in small groups may still permit inference, so the UI does not claim anonymity. Account deletion removes person-linked vote records according to F49.

## Failure and offline behavior
An offline selection is clearly pending and cannot count until acknowledged before closure. Retries with the same key return the same result; a new choice uses a new key and current vote version. Deadline racing a vote has one transactional outcome. If an attached recipe becomes unavailable, retain the author’s option label with an unavailable link; do not replace it automatically or alter votes. Missing workers cannot accept late votes.

## Integration and dependencies
F23 circles, F24 recipe rights, F33 Tonight, F34 pacts, F41 notifications, and F42 eligibility changes. Hard exclusions remain private and are applied only when a person adapts their chosen meal; votes must not publish those settings.

## Execution plan
Implement option/vote invariants and closure locking; build authoring/voting/results screens; add recipe context and personal planning; then integrate muted reminders, cancellation, and data cleanup. Observe late-vote rejections, closure lag, and duplicate suppression without individual vote analytics.

## Acceptance and release gates
Test simultaneous vote changes, closing-time equality, delayed workers, removed members, tied results, inaccessible recipes, and options edited after votes. `coordination.polls.enabled` stops new polls/votes while preserving cancellation and existing authorized results. Release requires exactly one vote per eligible member and no automatic pact, message, or purchase.
