Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026.

Who is responsible

FeedMe (com.anshhuman.feedme) is operated by Anshuman Acharya. For privacy requests, contact hazloteams3@gmail.com. This policy covers the connected FeedMe app and these public policy pages.

Information the connected app processes

  • Account and authentication: Supabase user identifier, Google-provided email and basic profile information, session/device identifiers, adult and Terms acceptance, and account status.
  • Cooking information: ingredients, pantry items, dietary and taste preferences, equipment, time, energy and cleanup choices, meal requests, recommendations, cooking progress, saves and feedback.
  • Optional social information: profile details, circles and membership, posts, captions, selected photos, messages, reactions, invitations, reports, blocks and chosen audiences.
  • Safety and operation: command identifiers, versions, security and moderation events, audit records, error or trace identifiers and bounded service-health information.
  • Support: your sender address, message and any attachments you choose when you email support.

FeedMe does not sell personal data or use third-party advertising SDKs in the first connected release.

Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate you; provide and restore cooking, Saved and intentional social features; enforce audiences and blocks; secure the service; investigate reports; operate account deletion and support; and comply with applicable obligations. Private cooking preferences are not used for advertising or public profiles.

AI processing

If AI request interpretation is enabled, FeedMe is designed to send Cloudflare Workers AI only bounded meal-request content and reviewed public ingredient options needed for that request. It is not designed to send your email, account identifier, social content, photos, medical history or an unrestricted copy of your pantry or profile for meal interpretation. You review the interpretation before it becomes a meal request.

If social-photo upload is enabled, FeedMe may separately send one decoded and re-encoded private display image to Cloudflare Workers AI solely for automated content moderation before the image can become available to its chosen audience. The moderation request is not designed to include your email or account identifier. An unavailable, malformed or uncertain result does not approve the image. FeedMe is designed to retain the bounded decision evidence needed for safety and audit, not the model's reasoning or a generated caption.

Cloudflare states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train AI models or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent. See Cloudflare's Workers AI data-usage documentation.

Service providers

  • Google provides Google sign-in and Google Play distribution.
  • Supabase provides authentication, PostgreSQL database and, when enabled, private media storage.
  • Render hosts the FeedMe application API.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI may process bounded meal-request content for request interpretation and one decoded, re-encoded social-photo derivative for automated content moderation, only when the relevant feature is enabled.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these public pages and processes ordinary web request information under GitHub's practices.
  • Email providers process support correspondence when you choose to send it.

These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing with other people

FeedMe shares social content with the audience you choose only when you intentionally publish or send it. Audience members can still copy or capture what they receive. Safety reports can expose limited content to authorized moderators. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Retention

Account, cooking and social information is kept while needed to provide your connected account, until you remove it through available controls or request account deletion, subject to feature-specific expiry and the limited exceptions below. Temporary links and Today content may expire sooner; Saved copies and messages have their own lifecycles.

Security, consent, moderation, deletion and transaction records may be retained for the limited time needed to protect users, preserve system integrity, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or meet legal obligations. Provider backups may take additional time to cycle after deletion. Access to retained records is restricted to their stated operational purpose.

Support messages are used to answer requests and retained only as needed for support or legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence at hazloteams3@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

You can edit supported profile and preference information, choose social audiences, remove supported content, block accounts, leave circles and request account deletion. The external account deletion page is available without reinstalling the app. Other rights may apply under the law where you live; contact support to exercise them.

Account deletion

You may start deletion in the app when that control is available or email a request through the external deletion page. FeedMe will verify that the request belongs to the account, confirm the expected completion window and delete or de-identify associated data, except information retained for a specifically stated security or legal reason. Temporary suspension is not treated as deletion.

Security

FeedMe uses encrypted transport, scoped server-side authorization, app-private storage and access controls. No system is completely secure. Do not send passwords, access tokens or highly sensitive medical information to support or social features. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Adults only

The connected FeedMe account service is intended only for people aged 18 years or older. It is not designed for children. If the operator learns that an ineligible person supplied personal data, contact support so access and deletion can be handled.

Changes and contact

Material changes will use a new effective date or policy version and, when required, renewed notice or consent. Contact Anshuman Acharya at hazloteams3@gmail.com.
