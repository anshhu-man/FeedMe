Delete your FeedMe account

You do not need to reinstall FeedMe to request deletion. FeedMe is operated by Anshuman Acharya.

In the app

When the in-app control is available, open Profile → Privacy → Delete account, review what deletion means and confirm with the required recent sign-in. If that control is unavailable, use the email path below.

Request deletion without the app

Email hazloteams3@gmail.com from the address associated with your FeedMe or Google sign-in, with the subject “FeedMe account deletion request.” Do not send your password, Google password, access token or one-time code.

Email a deletion request

FeedMe may ask for a safe verification step to prevent deletion of another person's account. The response will confirm the expected completion window and explain any information that must be retained for a stated security or legal reason.

What the request covers

The request covers your FeedMe account and associated profile, private cooking data, active sessions, Saved information and social content controlled by your account, with provider deletion requests where applicable. Independently authorized recipient copies or records retained for a specifically stated security or legal reason may be handled differently.

Support email and Google Play

Deleting FeedMe does not automatically delete email already sent to support or information held independently by Google Play or Google under their own services. Ask for deletion of support correspondence in the same message if desired.
