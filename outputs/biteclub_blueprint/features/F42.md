# F42 — Delete, Mute, Block, Report

## Strategy
People need direct control over their own posts, what they encounter, who can contact them, and how they request moderation. These four actions have different consequences and must not masquerade as one another. They ship before any social launch and remain reachable when social creation is disabled.

## Experience and screen design
`POST` overflow routes to `DELETE_POST`, mute, block, or `REPORT` according to ownership. `DELETE_POST` explains immediate post/media unavailability and the persistence of previously authorized recipe copies. `BLOCKED` lists owned block decisions with an Unblock action; unblocking explains that it does not restore old messages or send an announcement. `REPORT` asks for the target, reason category, and optional user-written context, then returns a receipt in `SUPPORT`. Reporting does not require publicly replying, and neither mute nor block sends a notice to the target.

## Production sequence
`DELETE /v1/posts/{postId}` with `If-Match` locks the owned post, writes a deletion tombstone and `post.deleted` in one transaction, revokes future content access, then queues private-object and derived-cache cleanup. `POST /v1/mutes` stores an owned target/type and `mute.created`; `DELETE /v1/mutes/{muteId}` removes it. `POST /v1/blocks` transactionally creates a unique blocker/target pair, increments authorization state, and writes `block.created` to cancel contact and media eligibility. `DELETE /v1/blocks/{userId}` removes that pair. `POST /v1/reports` validates a reportable target and commits a receipt plus `report.created` for the moderation queue. All durable actions use idempotency keys.

## Data and API contracts
`ContentTombstone(targetType,targetId,deletedBy,deletedAt,reason,version)`; `Mute(ownerId,targetType,targetId,createdAt)` with author/circle/thread targets; `Block(blockerId,targetId,createdAt)`; `Report(id,reporterId,targetType,targetId,reason,context,state,evidenceRef,createdAt)` define separate records. Self-blocking is invalid. Evidence is an access-controlled moderation snapshot with purpose and retention metadata, not a public copy or recipe grant. Reads are `GET /v1/blocks`, `GET /v1/mutes`, and owner-scoped `GET /v1/reports/{reportId}`. Errors include `NOT_OWNER`, `TARGET_UNAVAILABLE`, `REPORT_INVALID`, `VERSION_CONFLICT`, and `RATE_LIMITED`, with viewer-safe unavailability wording.

## Permissions and lifecycle
Delete denies new API reads and media capabilities at commit; purge is observable and retried. Existing signed media URLs retain F38’s residual TTL of at most 60 seconds, and started transfers may finish. Standard deletion preserves authorized private recipe copies; safety/legal recall is a separate audited operation. Mute hides selected feed/activity sources and pushes without revoking circle access. Block denies direct contact and new post/media capabilities in both directions even for stale clients. Moderators receive purpose-scoped evidence access. Unblocking restores neither removed memberships nor cancelled invitations. Account deletion follows F49’s redaction and retention rules.

## Failure and offline behavior
Mute can hide content locally while server state is pending. Block/delete show pending status until acknowledged and cannot claim remote enforcement offline. A timeout replays the same key or checks the owned tombstone. Failed purge jobs remain in retry/dead-letter queues; no new capability may be issued for tombstoned media, regardless of purge delay. Preserve unsent reports privately, but clear account-bound evidence references at logout. A report racing deletion can use a bounded prior access receipt without restoring content access.

## Integration and dependencies
Every social, conversation, coordination, media, and notification module consumes access invalidations and rechecks the source of truth. F30 recipe-copy persistence, F49 account deletion, F52 moderation appeals, and F54 audit/incident controls are required dependencies.

## Execution plan
Implement authorization-changing transactions and cleanup receipts; build distinct action copy and confirmation screens; connect scoped evidence and moderation queue; then add cross-module invalidation, recovery, abuse rate limits, and audit dashboards. Track purge lag, delivery denied after revocation, report queue age, and failed mutations.

## Acceptance and release gates
Test block/send and delete/save races, duplicate reports, cached media, deletion before reporting, ownership, mute without revoked access, and unblock without restored invitations. Feature kill switches never disable block, delete, report submission, or receipts. Release requires recoverable cleanup jobs, no new unauthorized API read/capability after commit, and accurate tests of the residual signed-URL window.
