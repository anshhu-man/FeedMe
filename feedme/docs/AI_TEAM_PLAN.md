## Parallel delivery order

1. **Build independent foundations together.** Platform completes retained control/startup lifetime ownership; cooking delivers the reviewed-input planner; social delivers membership/authorization persistence. Missing providers or content cannot become permissive test defaults.
2. **Connect real vertical journeys.** Wire approved identity to account/bootstrap, actual catalog/planning to Make Mine and cooking, and authorized circles/media to Today/My Plate. Preserve canonical operation IDs, request/response validation, version pins, rights, idempotency, offline recovery and recall behavior across boundaries.
3. **Rotate onto the remaining V1 packages.** Assign native inputs/onboarding, basic cookbook/memory, media/replies/notifications, moderation/editorial tools, billing and operational controls as their dependencies become usable. UI work reuses the approved visual direction and shared Kotlin; disconnected previews stay labelled as previews.
4. **Run release integration and pilot acceptance.** Exercise actual two-account journeys, physical devices, accessibility, privacy/deletion, outages, purchases or approved ads, signed artifacts and store requirements. A passing component or emulator demo never substitutes for these gates.

Milestones are not a strict waterfall. Provider-independent portions may start early, while the [release plan](RELEASE_PLAN.md) retains the prerequisites for accepting an entire feature. The team must not count test totals or assignment coverage as shipped capability.

## Definition of a finished package

- The canonical feature/screen/action/operation requirements and excluded later actions are identified before implementation.
- Production logic has one owned source of truth; fixtures, synthetic identities, local-only seams and missing integrations are explicit.
- Tests cover successful behavior, denial, stale/concurrent state, retries/cancellation and relevant offline/error cases with proportional native or database evidence.
- A different worker reviews the change; actual build/test failures are fixed without weakening the requirement or hiding failed evidence.
- The lead integrates the frozen files, checks compatibility and records exact artifact/source evidence and remaining work.
- Whole-feature, build-ready, store-ready and publicly-live acceptance remain separate. No automatic publishing or release approval follows from a green build.
