Finding a meal

List your ingredients, separated by commas. Pick your time, effort and cleanup preferences. FeedMe matches the names against its included recipes; it cannot check freshness, quantities or allergies. Review the listed amounts and missing ingredients before you cook.

No match? Check the ingredient names or change your limits. Some recipes specifically need fully cooked canned beans or ready-cooked rice; raw or dry ingredients are not interchangeable with those.

Saved recipes and clearing data

Saved recipes stay on the device where you saved them. There is no online account or cloud sync. Clear local data, followed by confirmation, removes your ingredient/preference draft and Saved choices. Uninstalling also removes the app's local files. Cleared choices cannot be recovered by FeedMe.

Offline cooking and Google Play

Meal matching, recipes and Saved choices work locally. Google Play installation verification may occasionally require a connection after the Play Store has been offline for a prolonged period. Read the installation-verification note for details.

Contact support

If something is not working, describe what happened and the steps you took. You may include your app version and Android version if helpful. Do not send passwords, health information or other sensitive details.
