# FeedMe asset provenance

The three PNGs in `shared/app/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable` are byte-for-byte copies of the existing approved food illustrations from the visual prototype: bowl, wrap and noodles.

Original generation prompts and provenance remain in the historical [asset manifest](../../outputs/biteclub_ui/ASSET_MANIFEST.md). They were not rewritten when the user renamed the project from BiteClub to FeedMe. These images illustrate the design; they do not establish recipe ingredients, nutritional values, safety, review status or completed cooking.

The active [visual prototype](../../outputs/biteclub_ui/index.html) and [blueprint](../../outputs/biteclub_blueprint/README.md) now display FeedMe. Their legacy directory paths remain stable for existing links. The new native workspace is `feedme/`. Historical ZIP archives retain their original names and contents.
