Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026.

Who operates FeedMe

FeedMe (com.anshhuman.feedme) is operated by Anshuman Acharya. For privacy questions or support, contact hazloteams3@gmail.com.

This policy covers the offline Android edition and these privacy/support pages. It does not describe a future connected service.

Information kept on your device

FeedMe remembers your ingredient and meal-preference draft and the identifiers of recipes you explicitly save. It uses these to restore your choices and show your Saved recipes. They are stored in the app's private on-device storage (Android SharedPreferences), not an online account.

FeedMe does not upload these choices or send them to Supabase, Render or another service. Recipe matching and cooking screens run on your device.

This edition has no sign-in, account creation, social posting, photo or camera features, advertising, purchases, analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not request Internet, camera, microphone, contacts or location access. It does not require an email address, date of birth or advertising identifier to use the cooking features.

Keeping and removing local information

Your draft and Saved recipe choices remain on this device until you change or remove them. Clear local data, followed by confirmation, removes both the draft and Saved recipe identifiers. Uninstalling FeedMe removes its local files.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app. FeedMe does not provide cloud sync or recovery of cleared choices.

App-private storage limits access by other ordinary apps. This is not a promise of protection against someone with access to your unlocked device or a compromised operating system, and it is not a claim of separately encrypted SharedPreferences.

Optional support email

Contact support opens your email app. Opening it does not send a message: you decide whether to send one and what to include. If you send an email, the operator receives your sender address, message and any attachments you choose through the email providers involved, including the operator's Gmail service.

FeedMe does not automatically attach your ingredient draft, Saved choices or diagnostic files. Avoid sending passwords, health information or other sensitive details. A parent or guardian can contact support on a child's behalf.

The operator uses support messages to answer requests and retains correspondence only as needed for support or legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing hazloteams3@gmail.com.

Clearing FeedMe's local data does not delete an email you sent. For questions about support correspondence or a request to delete it, email hazloteams3@gmail.com. External email providers handle their services under their own policies.

This website and external links

The app's public privacy-policy link opens your browser and may need Internet access. FeedMe does not include your local choices or a user identifier in that link.

When you visit these pages on GitHub Pages, that service hosts the website. The site includes no scripts, forms, external fonts or images, FeedMe analytics, or FeedMe tracking cookies. That does not mean the hosting service receives no information: GitHub says it records and keeps visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub.

GitHub handles hosting-related information under its own practices. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This policy does not promise a retention period for GitHub's logs.

Links to other websites are ordinary links, not embedded content. Following a link uses your browser and the destination's own services.

Younger users and cooking

The intended audience is ages 11 and above. FeedMe does not verify age or create child accounts. Children should cook with a responsible adult, particularly when using knives, heat or boiling water.

Check ingredient labels and allergies. Recipe suggestions are not medical advice or a guarantee that a meal is suitable for a particular person.
