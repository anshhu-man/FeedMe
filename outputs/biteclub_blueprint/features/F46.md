# F46 — Reviewed Situation Packs

Phase: later optional paid content. Owner modules: commerce, catalog; content operations in F51.

## Strategy

Offer professionally authored collections for practical situations, such as a small kitchen or minimal-preparation evenings. Each pack earns its value through reviewed usable content, not a promise of medical benefit. One-time pack ownership is the proposed starting model; a subscription requires a separate decision and demonstrated ongoing reviewed content production. No specific price is asserted.

## Experience and screen design

PACK_STORE lists published packs with a situation description and clear owned/available status. PACK_DETAIL shows included content, sample recipes, required equipment range, reviewer/provenance summary, supported preparation situations and purchase/access terms. Try a free sample opens RECIPE. Unlock opens PAYWALL with the current localized store price and billing type. PURCHASE_STATUS distinguishes pending, canceled, failed and confirmed access. Open owned pack navigates to COLLECTION as a read-only pack collection; users can save eligible recipes into their own cookbook. A pack never bypasses personal exclusions simply because it was purchased.

## Production sequence

1. Authors build a versioned manifest from individually reviewed published recipe/variant IDs and editorial context.
2. Content review confirms claims, serving bounds, substitutions and effort; commerce validates canonical product mappings and platform availability.
3. Publish catalog/offer metadata atomically with an outbox event only when both content and store readiness gates pass.
4. After explicit purchase/restore, F50 reconciles authoritative entitlement. Pack delivery authorizes the account and pins the entitled manifest/version rights.
5. Updates publish new manifests. Withdrawal from sale stops new offers but preserves owned access; recalls independently disable affected content and explain permitted replacement/refund support routes without promising automatic remedies.

## Data and API contracts

GET /v1/packs lists published metadata; GET /v1/packs/{packId} returns public preview or authorized owned manifest according to principal rights. POST /v1/entitlements/reconcile refreshes purchase access. POST /v1/admin/packs creates drafts; POST /v1/admin/packs/{packId}/publish and /withdraw perform role-authorized audited lifecycle operations. Product purchase itself uses the native billing integration through F50, never a trusted client paid field.

Internal commerce/catalog records retain immutable manifest versions, reviewed claims, locale coverage, product mappings, offer rights and withdrawal/recall state; they are not arbitrary extra Pack JSON keys. Store offerings supply current price/billing details, and catalog entitlement checks identify permitted samples. Paid downloads use authorized manifests with hashes, not permanent public content URLs.

## Permissions and lifecycle

Purchase terms must explicitly state the included version/update rights before sale. Proposed one-time access covers the purchased pack's licensed content; future separately sold packs are not implied. Existing authorized basic saved recipe copies remain accessible under their grant after offer changes, except documented safety/legal recall. A purchase does not transfer authorship or grant public redistribution. Identity deletion and billing cancellation are separate processes explained through F49/F50. Household access applies only if the offering explicitly grants supported member access.

## Failure and offline behavior

Do not show a guessed price when the store metadata is unavailable; retain previews and offer Retry. Pending purchases cannot unlock protected downloads until reconciliation confirms rights. Download manifests and recipe bundles atomically; retry missing assets without charging again. Owned downloaded content is usable offline under its established rights, subject to recall synchronization limits. Store-account/app-account restore conflicts go to an explicit recovery state, not silent ownership reassignment. A recalled recipe in a pack remains visibly unavailable while unaffected recipes stay usable.

## Integration and dependencies

F13/F51 review/versioning, F19 permitted saving, F50 billing, F04 personalized filters and F53 offline delivery. F45 organization is a distinct offering; owning a pack need not require a library upgrade. F54 tracks delivery and recall reliability.

## Execution plan

- Define manifest/product mapping schema and publish/withdraw transactions.
- Build PACK_STORE/PACK_DETAIL with authoritative offer metadata and sample routing.
- Integrate entitlement checks, owned collection delivery and atomic downloads.
- Add recall propagation across manifest dependencies; emit pack_access_denied, pack_download_failed and pack_recall_affected.

## Acceptance and release gates

Test unsigned entitlement assertions, pending/duplicate purchases, restore conflicts, product rename, withdrawn offers with existing owners, mixed reviewed/unreviewed manifests, content recall and partial downloads. Every sold pack needs completed editorial review and platform billing acceptance; no live sale follows from this blueprint alone. Kill switches stop pack_sales or a specific pack independently of owned access and recall enforcement.
