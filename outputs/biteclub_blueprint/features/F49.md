# F49 — Account data and privacy operations

## Strategy
Provide understandable in-app access to export, session control and account deletion. Users should understand the difference between private preferences, their media, previously permitted recipe copies and store billing.

## Experience and screen design
PRIVACY links to BLOCKED, EXPORT, MEMORY, LEGAL and DELETE_ACCOUNT. EXPORT shows requested/processing/ready/expired status. DELETE_ACCOUNT requires recent authentication and explicit confirmation, explains immediate access loss and documented retention, and links to MANAGE_PLAN without blocking the deletion request. SESSIONS identifies devices approximately and exposes selected/all-session revocation.

## Production sequence
An export command authenticates recent identity, creates a unique active job and writes an outbox event. Worker snapshots owned data with role/third-party redaction, encrypts the archive, stores it in a private bucket and records a short expiration. A fresh authenticated read issues a short-lived download capability. A deletion command immediately marks the account `deletion_pending`, prevents new social access and revokes sessions. A durable job then tombstones/removes owned posts/media, removes memberships, processes conversations and permitted copies according to policy, and deletes identity mappings after required operational completion.

## Data and API contracts
`POST /v1/account/exports` returns `Accepted.jobId`; poll `GET /v1/jobs/{jobId}` for `Job.status` and, when ready, its authorized `downloadUrl` and `downloadExpiresAt`. Account deletion uses `POST /v1/account/deletion`; session management uses the account-session routes. Tables: account_jobs with type/state/cursor/policyVersion and unique active-job constraint, export_artifacts with expiration and encrypted object reference, retention_holds with authorized purpose. Each job has retry cursor and receipt. A deletion job's parts are idempotent; never re-create an account from a delayed purchase webhook. After session revocation, completion status is communicated through the approved deletion receipt/support process; the revoked session cannot poll a protected job endpoint.

## Permissions and lifecycle
Recent-auth policy is server-checked. Export links are bearer capabilities with a bounded lifetime and cannot be put in push payloads. Proposed normal objectives are immediate access suspension, export availability for 24 hours, and active-data deletion within 24 hours, matching the production runbook. Backups, restricted moderation evidence and transaction records may have separately justified, approved retention exceptions; these are disclosed rather than silently included in the normal completion claim. No measured service guarantee or legal sufficiency is asserted. Ordinary post deletion leaves prior authorized recipe-only copies intact; account erasure removes creator identity and shared UGC and recalls personal user-authored copies wherever continued retention is not demonstrably permitted. Independently licensed catalog or recipient-authored content may remain with anonymized provenance. Source photos/comments never persist through a recipe-copy grant.

## Failure and offline behavior
Queue outages leave a durable pending job, not a false success. Worker replay resumes at the stored cursor. Expired exports require a new request. Offline deletion cannot be claimed accepted: retain no typed confirmation beyond the session, show reconnect and a support path. A deleted account's old access token must fail the application account-state gate even before its cryptographic expiry.

## Integration and dependencies
Identity, media, circles, social, conversations, cookbook, commerce and analytics have explicit deletion handlers. RevenueCat/store subscription cancellation is separate from application account deletion and is explained in UI. F54 tracks completion deadlines and queue age without including export content.

## Execution plan
Create a data retention inventory and per-module export/delete interfaces. Implement account-state gate before module endpoints, orchestration/outbox jobs, download authorization and deletion receipts. Dry-run with synthetic accounts, shared posts, blocked users, saved copies, household memberships and pending purchases. Verify backup restore replays deletion tombstones before access is reopened.

## Acceptance and release gates
Test lost request responses, worker death halfway through purge, duplicate webhooks after deletion, expired export downloads and unauthorized export IDs. Confirm external-source data is redacted correctly and every owned-data table has a handler. Release requires an approved retention policy, store-policy check, deletion runbook and audited restore exercise. Kill new exports independently if packaging leaks data; do not disable account deletion acceptance.
